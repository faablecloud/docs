# Challenge pricing

Faable Challenge is **included in every Faable plan**. There is nothing to add to your subscription and no metered line on your invoice: each plan includes a monthly allowance of verifications and a number of sites, **per project**.

| Plan                   | Verifications a month | Sites     |
| ---------------------- | --------------------- | --------- |
| **Free** (0 €)         | 1,000                 | 1         |
| **Hobby** (15 €/month) | 10,000                | 5         |
| **Pro** (99 €/month)   | 100,000               | Unlimited |

The plan is the project's; see [Pricing](/platform/pricing) for everything else each plan includes.

## What counts

- **A successful verify counts.** Each `POST /v1/verify` that answers `success: true` is one verification — and so is a `challenge_token` that [Faable Auth verifies](/challenge/faable-auth#with-the-token) for you.
- **Challenges issued do not count**, and neither do failed verifies. A script that asks for challenges and never solves them does not spend your allowance.
- **Faable Auth's own protection never counts.** [Signup Protection](/auth/signup-protection) on your Auth clients is included with Faable Auth, whatever its volume.
- The count is per calendar month and per project, across all the project's sites.

## Going over

**Verification keeps working above the allowance.** A form protected by Faable Challenge never starts failing because of the plan. The people who pay for the project get an email at 80 % and at 100 % of the month's verifications, the dashboard shows the project over its plan, and both suggest moving to the next one.

Sites are a hard limit: creating a site beyond what the plan includes is refused with `402 plan_required` until the project moves up a plan. Existing sites keep working.

## Where to see it

**Challenge** in the [Faable Dashboard](https://dashboard.faable.com) shows this month's verifications against the plan, the checks issued, the failed verifications, and how many sites the project has of how many it may.
