# Auth0 alternative: Faable Auth vs Auth0

**Short answer:** Auth0 is the reference managed identity platform: OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect, a deep feature set and the most complete tooling in the category. It is priced **per monthly active user**, in tiers, with B2B features on a separate, more expensive price list. Faable Auth is a managed OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect identity server in the same category, and it **never charges per user**. It is free up to 15,000 MAU with no credit card, and **Hobby (15 €/month) and Pro (99 €/month) have no MAU limit**. It's operated **in Europe by a European company**, and it's the **same subscription as your app hosting** on [Faable Deploy](../deploy/get-started.md).

> Auth0 details checked against auth0.com pricing and docs in November 2026. They change often, so confirm anything decisive on their own site.

## Pricing, worked through

Auth0 publishes its prices as a table of MAU tiers, and "if your usage falls between tiers, you are billed at the next tier up".

| Monthly active users | Auth0 B2C Essentials | Auth0 B2C Professional | Auth0 B2B Essentials | Auth0 B2B Professional | **Faable Auth**                   |
| -------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 1,000                | $70                  | $240                   | $300                 | $800                   | **0 €** (Free)                    |
| 5,000                | $350                 | $1,000                 | $1,300               | $1,500                 | **0 €** (Free)                    |
| 10,000               | $700                 | $1,600                 | $2,100               | $2,400                 | **0 €** (Free)                    |
| 20,000               | $1,400               | $3,200                 | $3,800               | Contact sales          | **15 €** (Hobby) · **99 €** (Pro) |
| 50,000               | $3,500               | Contact sales          | Contact sales        | Contact sales          | **15 €** (Hobby) · **99 €** (Pro) |

Monthly prices, as Auth0 lists them. Auth0's free tier covers the first 25,000 MAU; the table shows what you pay once you need a paid feature such as role management, more Actions or log streaming.

On top of that come the add-ons: more enterprise connections at $100/month each, M2M token packs, and enterprise MFA on B2B Essentials at $100/month. On Faable the plan fee is the bill. The only usage lines are M2M tokens beyond 100 a month (€2.25 per 1,000), enterprise SSO users beyond 50 a month on Pro, and recovery SMS. See [Auth pricing](pricing.mdx).

**The reading:** under 25,000 users with no paid features, Auth0's free tier is generous, and more generous than ours at 15,000. Once you need a paid feature or pass 25,000 users, Auth0's price rises with every tier while Faable's stays at 15 € or 99 €. The gap is largest for B2B, where Auth0's separate price list starts at $150/month.

## B2B: organizations and enterprise SSO

Auth0's Free plan includes 5 organizations and 1 enterprise connection. The B2B tiers make organizations unlimited and include 3 (Essentials) or 5 (Professional) enterprise connections, at $100/month for each one above that, up to 30.

On Faable Pro, every customer can be an [organization](organizations.md) with verified email domains, its own identity provider (Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace or any OpenID Connect provider) and [auto-join](jit-provisioning.mdx) for new employees. There's no per-connection fee: 50 federated users a month are included, and each one above that costs €0.0010. Your tenant can also be the [SAML identity provider](saml.md) of your own apps. Auth0 supports SAML in both directions, and Faable doesn't accept inbound SAML connections yet: if your customers' identity providers only speak SAML, that matters.

## Getting your users out

Leaving any identity provider means taking the password hashes with you, or your users reset their passwords.

- **Auth0** exports password hashes and MFA secrets through a **support case**: Auth0 reviews eligibility ("not all requests qualify"), a second tenant admin must confirm, and an acknowledgment form has to be signed by a CISO, CSO or VP-level executive. The download link expires after three days. Bulk user exports without hashes are self-service, but not on the Free plan.
- **Faable Auth** imports bcrypt, scrypt, PBKDF2 and Argon2id hashes, and exports them, on every plan, with `faable auth users import` and `faable auth users export --include-hashes`. See [Import and export password hashes](guides/import-password-hashes.mdx).

## Where your users' data lives

Auth0 lets you choose the region of your tenant, including one in the EU, and it's part of Okta, a US company. Faable Auth runs **in Europe**, operated by a European company, with no non-EU region. For SOC 2 or ISO 27001 questions, see our [compliance scope](../platform/compliance.md#compliance-scope).

## When Auth0 might fit better

- **You need the deepest feature set**: adaptive MFA, SMS sign-in, inbound SAML, bot detection and a large integration marketplace.
- **You manage identity as code**, with Terraform and the Deploy CLI.
- **Your company is standardized on Okta**, and Auth0 fits that procurement and tooling.
- **You stay under 25,000 MAU on free features**, where Auth0 costs nothing and gives you more headroom than our Free plan.

## When Faable Auth is the better fit

- **You're past Auth0's free tier**, or need one paid feature, and want a bill that doesn't grow with your users.
- **You're building B2B** and don't want organizations and SSO connections priced as add-ons.
- **You want a European company**, not just a European region.
- **You want auth and hosting together.** [Faable Deploy](../deploy/get-started.md) and Faable Auth share one [subscription](pricing.mdx).

## Migrating from Auth0

The concepts map one to one: Auth0 tenant → Faable account, application → client, API → API, Action → Action. Social connections become [Connections](connections.md) with your existing upstream credentials, and users keep their passwords once you have the hash export. The step-by-step version is in **[Migrate from Auth0](guides/migrate-from-auth0.mdx)**.
