# Faable Auth

Faable Auth signs your users in so you do not have to build it: a hosted login screen, social and passwordless sign-in, passkeys and two-step verification, on standard OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Each project gets its own isolated tenant, hosted 100% in Europe.
