# Support

## Contact

- **Email:** [support@faable.com](mailto:support@faable.com) — included in the Hobby and Pro plans.
- **Community:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/faablecloud/discussions) — for every plan, including Free.

Response times are not contractually guaranteed on any plan; see [Pricing](pricing.md#support).

## Before you write

Most deploy problems are explained by the build or the runtime logs, which you can see in the dashboard or with `faable deploy logs`. These pages cover the common ones:

- [Build failures](../deploy/build-failures.md) — why a build fails and how to fix it.
- [Errors](../deploy/errors.md) — what each error code means.
- [FAQ](../faq.mdx)

## What to include

Write from the email address on your Faable account, and include:

- the **project** and the **app** (their names, or the IDs from the dashboard URL);
- the **deployment ID** if it is about a build or a deploy;
- **what you expected and what happened**, with the time it happened (and your time zone);
- for billing, the **invoice number**.

## Billing and refunds

Questions about charges, invoices, VAT, or refunds go to [support@faable.com](mailto:support@faable.com) too. What we refund, and how, is in the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md).

## Other contacts

- **Privacy and data protection:** [privacy@faable.com](mailto:privacy@faable.com) — see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
- **Abuse hosted on Faable:** [abuse@faable.com](mailto:abuse@faable.com) — see [how to report abuse](security-compliance.md).
- **Service availability on Pro:** the [SLA](sla.md), and how to claim service credits.
