# Pricing

Faable is sold as a **single subscription that covers every product** — both Faable Deploy (compute) and Faable Auth (identity). Pick the platform tier that matches the stage you're at, and you're done.

Each tier is a **fixed monthly fee** that unlocks features and entitlements, and everything the tier includes — instances, deployments, users, egress — comes with the fee. There is never a per-user charge.

**Three things are metered, only above what the plan includes:**

- **Egress, on Pro:** 100 GB included, then 0.18 €/GB. Free and Hobby are not metered — their allowance (10 GB and 50 GB) is what the tier includes, and no per-GB line can appear on their invoice. See [Deploy pricing](../deploy/pricing.md#bandwidth).
- **Recovery SMS, on Hobby and Pro:** 20 a month included, then per message. See [Auth pricing](../auth/pricing.mdx#sms).
- **Machine-to-machine tokens, on Hobby and Pro:** 100 a month included, then €2.25 per 1,000. Free is capped at 100 and never needs a card. See [Auth pricing](../auth/pricing.mdx#machine-to-machine-tokens).

Plans apply **per project**: each project has its own plan, starting on Free. An account can keep
**up to 3 projects on Free** — 2 during its first 48 hours — and moving any one of them to Hobby
frees a slot for another.

## Plans

| Plan      | Monthly fee | What you get                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free**  | 0 €         | Try Faable end-to-end, no credit card. Up to 3 projects per account, 1 free instance, up to 10 successful deployments per day, build artifacts up to 512 MB, 10 GB egress / month, Faable Auth with up to 15,000 MAU, community support. |
| **Hobby** | 15 €        | Production-ready for a single product. Unlimited MAU, full instance catalog, unlimited deployments, build artifacts up to 2 GB, 50 GB egress / month, custom domains, email support.                                                     |
| **Pro**   | 99 €        | Unlimited MAU, custom domain for your login, multiple auth accounts, 100 GB egress / month plus metered traffic beyond it, audit logs, 99.9 % uptime SLA.                                                                                |

The fee unlocks the tier; the metered lines above are the only thing that can be added to it. The deep-dives below break down exactly what each plan includes per product.

## What's covered by your plan

The platform tier you choose maps to concrete entitlements in each product. See:

- **[Faable Auth pricing](../auth/pricing.md)** — MAU allowances, auth accounts, audit logs.
- **[Faable Deploy pricing](../deploy/pricing.md)** — compute instance catalog (`bi.xs` … `bi.2xlarge`), deployments per day, build artifact size limits, and the monthly egress allowance of each plan.

## Support

Support is included in your plan — there is nothing extra to buy.

- **Free** — [community support](https://github.com/orgs/faablecloud/discussions).
- **Hobby and Pro** — email support at [support@faable.com](mailto:support@faable.com).

Pro additionally carries a 99.9 % uptime SLA. Response times are not contractually guaranteed on any tier; if you need one, [talk to us](mailto:support@faable.com).

## Billing

- Billing cycle is **monthly, in arrears** — at the start of each month you receive the invoice for the previous month's usage.
- All prices are in **EUR** and shown **without taxes (VAT)** included.
- Payment methods are managed from your account's **Billing** settings in the dashboard; upgrades go through a secure hosted checkout.
