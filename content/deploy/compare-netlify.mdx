# Netlify alternative: Faable Deploy vs Netlify

**Short answer:** Netlify is a frontend platform: push to Git, get your site on a global CDN with deploy previews, forms and functions for the dynamic parts. Since its move to credit-based plans, every production deploy, gigabyte of bandwidth, request and minute of compute draws from a monthly credit balance. On the Free plan, when the credits run out, **every site is paused** until the next cycle. Faable Deploy keeps the same Git workflow with **a fixed monthly fee per project**, no meters on Free and Hobby, and **a free plan that keeps serving your sites**. Your backend runs as **a real, long-running server**, **[Faable Auth](../auth/get-started.md) is included**, and **your apps and data are hosted in the EU** by a European company.

> Netlify details checked against netlify.com pricing and docs in November 2026. They change often, so confirm anything decisive on their own site.

## Why teams look for a Netlify alternative

### Credits turn every deploy into a cost

On Netlify's credit plans, a production deploy costs 15 credits, a gigabyte of bandwidth 20, a GB-hour of compute 10, and 10,000 requests 2. The Free plan's 300 credits a month are about **20 production deploys**, or **15 GB of bandwidth**, before anything else. Paid plans can buy more credits, at $5 per 500 on Personal and $10 per 1,500 on Pro. Free has a hard limit: once the balance is spent, "all of your web projects (sites/apps) are paused", visitors see a "Site not available" page, and new production deploys are blocked until the cycle resets.

Faable charges **a fixed fee per plan, per project, never per user**, and there's no meter on Free or Hobby. The [Free plan](pricing.mdx) allows up to 50 deployments a day per project, and a push over the daily quota is **queued, not dropped**. Failed builds never count. Going over the 10 GB traffic allowance sends warnings at 80 %, 90 % and 100 %; it doesn't take your site offline. Hobby is **15 €/month** with unlimited deployments and 50 GB of egress; only Pro meters egress beyond its 100 GB. See [Deploy pricing](pricing.mdx).

### A backend that isn't a function

Netlify runs dynamic code as functions: 60 seconds for a synchronous request, 30 seconds for a scheduled one and 15 minutes in the background, with 1 GB of memory by default. A long-running server option, Netlify Server, is in early access, and full-stack frameworks still run as functions.

On Faable your code runs as **an ordinary server process** in a Linux container: Next.js with `next start`, Astro, Express, Fastify, NestJS, Django, FastAPI, Flask, Laravel or plain PHP, detected and built without configuration. The only timeout is 60 seconds to the first byte, after which a response can stream for minutes (see [Hosting AI agents in Europe](ai-agents.mdx)). On Hobby and Pro, [background workers](always-on-and-workers.md) run processes with no web server, and [scheduled tasks](scheduled-tasks.md) run any command of your app on a cron schedule, for as long as it needs. Static sites are served directly, with no container and no cold start.

### Auth is in the same subscription

Most sites on Netlify add a second vendor for sign-in, with its own bill and its own data-processing agreement. On Faable, [Faable Auth](../auth/get-started.md) is part of the plan: hosted login, social and passwordless sign-in, MFA, passkeys and machine-to-machine tokens, with **15,000 monthly active users on Free** and no MAU limit on Hobby and Pro.

### European by construction

Netlify deploys functions to Ohio by default, and choosing a European region is a per-function setting on Pro. Faable is a European company: **your apps and data are hosted in the EU**, with hosting and identity under one provider and one data-processing agreement. More in [European alternatives to Vercel, Render and Railway](european-alternatives.mdx).

## When Netlify might fit better

- **Your site is static or Jamstack, with a global audience**, and you want it on a worldwide CDN.
- **You rely on deploy previews for every branch and pull request.** They cost no credits on Netlify; Faable doesn't create them, though you can deploy a staging app from [your own CI](github-actions.md).
- **You use Netlify Forms or edge functions** as part of your site.

## Moving from Netlify

Connect the same repository, copy your environment variables, deploy, and point your domain at Faable. Static builds are served as they are; a `_redirects` file is handled in your framework or server instead. The step-by-step version is in **[Migrate from Netlify](guides/migrate-from-netlify.mdx)**.
