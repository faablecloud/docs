# Faable Deploy vs Vercel, Render & Railway

**Short answer:** Faable Deploy gives you the same push-to-deploy workflow as Vercel, Render and Railway — connect a GitHub repository and every push ships — and adds what the others sell separately or not at all: a **Web Application Firewall on every plan**, **[Faable Auth](../auth/get-started.md) in the same subscription**, a **permanent free plan that allows commercial use and custom domains**, a **fixed monthly fee in EUR, never per user**, and **100% European hosting with full GDPR data sovereignty**.

If you are choosing a platform for a real product — a SaaS backend, an API, an AI agent, a client's site — that combination means one vendor, one bill and one data-processing agreement instead of three.

## Why teams pick Faable Deploy

### Your free plan can run a real business

Vercel's Hobby plan is for **personal, non-commercial use only** — a client landing page or a side project that charges money is obliged to move to Pro, however little traffic it gets. Railway replaced its free tier with **trial credit that runs out**, and its free plan has no custom domains. Render's free web services **take around a minute to wake up**.

Faable's [Free plan](pricing.mdx) is permanent, needs no credit card, **allows commercial use**, includes **custom domains**, and gives each project an instance with 0.5 CPU and 1 GB of memory — double Render's free 512 MB. An idle Free app wakes in a few seconds, and static sites are served directly with no container at all, so they never have a cold start.

### Security and identity are included, not assembled

Every app sits behind a [managed Web Application Firewall](security-waf.md) running the OWASP Core Rule Set — on every plan, including Free. On Vercel a WAF with custom rules is a paid feature; on Render and Railway it is something you put in front yourself.

And when your app needs users, [Faable Auth](../auth/get-started.md) is already in your subscription: hosted login, social and passwordless login, MFA, passkeys and machine-to-machine tokens, with **15,000 monthly active users on the Free plan**. On the other platforms that is a second vendor — Auth0, Clerk or Cognito — with a second bill and a second data-processing agreement.

### Pricing you can predict

Faable charges a **fixed monthly fee per plan, per project — never per user or per seat**. Hobby is 15 €/month with unlimited deployments and unlimited monthly active users. On Free and Hobby there is no per-GB line at all: traffic is included, and only Pro meters egress beyond its 100 GB (0.18 €/GB). Compare that with Vercel's per-seat Pro, Render's plan fee plus compute, and Railway's usage-based billing — all in USD.

The Free plan also fails gracefully: hit the daily deployment quota and your latest push is **queued, not dropped**; a failed build never counts against it; and going over the egress allowance sends you warnings at 80 %, 90 % and 100 % instead of pausing your site. See [Deploy pricing](pricing.mdx).

### European by construction, not by region

Choosing an EU region on a US platform keeps your data's location in Europe, but the company operating it — and the law that can reach it — stays American. Faable is a European company running on European infrastructure: **hosting is 100% European with full GDPR data sovereignty**, and your hosting and identity data sit under one European provider. For many procurement and compliance reviews, that is the difference between "acceptable" and "approved". More on this in [European alternatives to Vercel, Render and Railway](european-alternatives.mdx).

### Built for backends and AI agents

Your code runs as a real server process in a Linux container — Django, FastAPI, Flask, Express, Fastify, NestJS, Next.js SSR, Laravel or plain PHP — detected and built with zero configuration; a Dockerfile is the escape hatch on Hobby and Pro. Responses can **stream for minutes**: the only timeout is 60 seconds to the first byte, which is what LLM apps need (see [Hosting AI agents in Europe](ai-agents.mdx)). And with the [Faable MCP server](../cli.md#mcp-server), your coding agent can deploy, read build logs and fix a failing release without you copying anything into the chat.

### A human answers

Hobby and Pro get email support straight from **the engineers who build and run the platform** — not a ticket queue in front of a bot.

## When another tool might fit better

- **Vercel** if you are all-in on Next.js edge rendering and per-branch preview deployments, and neither commercial use on a free plan nor European hosting matters to you.
- **Render** if you need a managed Postgres, cron jobs and background workers from the same provider. On Faable, connect an [EU-hosted database such as Neon or Turso](guides/databases.md) in minutes.
- **Railway** if you want to assemble databases and services from a visual canvas and prefer pure usage-based billing.

## How deploying works on Faable

Connect your GitHub repository, push your code, and Faable detects your stack, builds it, and serves it at `https://<app>.faable.link` with free SSL — no Dockerfiles, YAML or servers to provision. Deploys from your own CI work too, via [GitHub Actions](github-actions.md) or the [CLI](../cli.md).
