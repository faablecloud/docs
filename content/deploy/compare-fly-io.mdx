# Faable Deploy vs Fly.io

**Short answer:** Fly.io is infrastructure you configure; Faable Deploy is a platform that configures itself. On Fly.io you write a `fly.toml`, size machines, pick regions and pay for each of them by the second. On Faable you push to GitHub and your app is live — built, served over SSL, behind a **Web Application Firewall**, with **[Faable Auth](../auth/get-started.md) in the same subscription**, on a **permanent free plan** or a **fixed monthly fee in EUR**, and **100% hosted in Europe under GDPR**.

## Why teams pick Faable Deploy over Fly.io

### Nothing to configure

There is no `fly.toml`, no machine size to choose and no region list to maintain. Faable detects Django, FastAPI, Flask, Express, Fastify, NestJS, Next.js, Astro, Vite, Laravel and plain PHP from your repository, builds them, and serves the result at `https://<app>.faable.link` — see [What is zero-config CI/CD?](what-is-zero-config-cicd.mdx). Every push to your deploy branch ships the same way, from the GitHub integration, [your own CI](github-actions.md) or the [CLI](../cli.md).

### A bill you know in advance

Fly.io bills every running machine, volume, dedicated IPv4 address and gigabyte of egress, in USD. That is fair for infrastructure, but it means your invoice depends on how you sized things last month. Faable charges a **fixed monthly fee per project, never per user** — 15 €/month on Hobby with unlimited deployments, 50 GB of egress and Faable Auth with unlimited monthly active users. Free and Hobby never bill per GB. See [Deploy pricing](pricing.mdx).

### A free plan that does not expire

Fly.io replaced its free allowance with a short trial. Faable's [Free plan](pricing.mdx) is permanent and needs no credit card: up to 3 projects, an instance per project, custom domains, 50 deployments a day and 10 GB of egress a month. Idle apps sleep after 30 minutes and wake on the next request in a few seconds; static sites are served with no container and no cold start at all.

### Security and identity included

Every app is protected by a [managed Web Application Firewall](security-waf.md) running the OWASP Core Rule Set — no proxy to deploy in front of your app. When you need sign-in, [Faable Auth](../auth/get-started.md) gives you hosted login, social and passwordless login, MFA, passkeys and machine-to-machine tokens in the same account.

### European company, European infrastructure

Fly.io runs machines in European regions, but it is a US company. Faable is European end to end: **hosting is 100% European with full GDPR data sovereignty**, and your hosting and identity data sit with one European provider under one data-processing agreement. Support on Hobby and Pro comes directly from the engineers who build the platform — no support plan to buy.

### Ready for AI apps and agents

Streaming responses can run for minutes — the only limit is 60 seconds to the first byte — and [HTTP/3](http3.md) is on by default. See [Hosting AI agents in Europe](ai-agents.mdx), and let your coding agent manage deploys through the [Faable MCP server](../cli.md#mcp-server).

## When Fly.io might fit better

- You need to place workloads in **specific regions around the world** and route between them yourself.
- You run **persistent volumes, long-running workers or custom networking** and want to manage machines directly.
