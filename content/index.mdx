# Faable Documentation

**Faable** is a European cloud platform with two products in one account: **Faable Deploy**
to build and host your apps, and **Faable Auth** to sign in their users. Pick the one you
are working with — each has its own documentation.

## Across the platform

- **[Faable CLI](/cli.md)** — deploy, logs, secrets and domains, and Faable Auth users and
  clients, from the terminal.
- **[Pricing](/platform/pricing.mdx)** — one plan covers both products.
- **[Security & Compliance](/platform/security-compliance.md)** — EU hosting, GDPR and how
  your data is protected.
- **[FAQ](/faq.mdx)** — direct answers to the most common questions.
